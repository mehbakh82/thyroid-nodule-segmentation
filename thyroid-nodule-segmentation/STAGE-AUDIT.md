## Fixed method combination and matched comparator

Use the original grayscale and contrast-only checkpoints. For each image, average their foreground probabilities (50/50) and threshold at .5. Compare against the grayscale model run twice, once normally and once on a horizontally flipped input, unflipping the probability map and averaging. Both methods use two forward passes, although their model storage and preprocessing costs differ. Secondary check: a single 3×3 elliptical morphological closing in the valid image field, without component filtering.

| Method | Mean Dice | Surface Dice at 2 px | Mean HD95 (px) | Complete misses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Grayscale, single pass | .8150 | .4875 | 18.33 | 9 |
| Contrast, single pass | .8156 | .4810 | 19.51 | 8 |
| Gray + contrast, two-model mean | .8200 | .4912 | 17.52 | 7 |
| Gray + horizontally flipped gray, single model | **.8221** | **.4938** | 17.80 | 7 |

The two-view grayscale baseline gained **0.71 Dice points** over single-pass grayscale (paired 95% image-bootstrap interval +0.38 to +1.10). The two-model ensemble gained **0.51 points** over single-pass grayscale, yet scored **0.20 points lower** than the two-pass grayscale baseline (interval −0.61 to +0.19). The ensemble had numerically lower average HD95. Morphological closing changed mean Dice by less than 0.002 points for either tested input. CPU-versus-MPS replay differed by one thresholded pixel in one image per single arm. Maximum single-image Dice deviation was below 0.00014. All fusion comparisons use consistent CPU inference. Future development should include flip averaging as a low-effort comparator when appropriate. [Paired results](results/fusion-audit.json) · [Inference and aggregation](fusion_audit.py)

## Can erasing calipers explain when contrast hurts?

Used the 20 previously marked cross centers across seven selected development images. Inpainted four-pixel disks with the Telea algorithm, then compared with three equally sized nearby edits per image selected to approximately match foreground/background composition. The control selection uses the annotation only for this experimental comparison. Measure change on the original fixed reference. A disk can erase tissue as well as part of a cross, and can leave longer caliper lines. Sham edits may also contain genuine structure.

For contrast, real edits scored **2.65 Dice points above control edits on average**, median +0.86. Omitting the single largest contributor changed the mean to **−0.78**. For grayscale the corresponding mean was −0.28. This supports retaining markers as a question for the reader. It does not establish a reliable subgroup effect or justify training an inpainting pipeline as the main thesis. [Case-level diagnostic](results/mark-intervention.json) · [Li et al., marker-mask inpainting](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2023.12.011) · [Prior caliper-guided segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2019.10.003)

## Which creative direction merits investment?

1. **If the important error is choosing the wrong structure**, compare a gland-context or localization-first approach with the grayscale flip baseline. TRFE and cascaded detection/segmentation are prior art. First count independently judged wrong-target errors and establish how many have a recoverable target. Decide whether a user prompt is part of the intended workflow. [TRFE](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2022.106389) · [Multitask detection and segmentation](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11354840/)
2. **If the important error is tracing an identifiable nodule**, the most distinctive candidate here is to test local corrections *only where a documented reference convention and a blinded second reader support the improvement*. Use the rest of the contour as a preservation control. The JPEG decoder disagreement identifies a technical sensitivity; it cannot label anatomical ambiguity. Compare with fixed flip averaging, a capacity-matched generic refinement, and a published boundary-aware method. Expert-labelled ambiguous arcs, estimates of their frequency on fresh cases, and an overlap/precision calculation would determine whether to advance. Uncertainty-guided residual refinement, spatial label smoothing, and multi-reader learning already have precedents. The contribution must be a demonstrable improvement for the specified thyroid failure. [Thyroid boundary refinement](https://doi.org/10.3389/fcell.2026.1879094) · [Multireader DiFF](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2024.3394045) · [Spatial label smoothing](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9974964/)
3. **If calipers are part of the workflow**, explicitly compare automatic and caliper-guided segmentation with matched inference information. If calipers should be absent, prefer naturally clean external images or naturally paired acquisitions. Inpainted scans alone have treatment artifacts. Scope depends on the lab’s acquisition workflow and access. [TUD](https://github.com/NEU-LX/TUD-Datebase) · [ThyroidXL](https://github.com/VNPT-AI-Official/ThyroidXL)

## Decision at the meeting

Start with the blinded cases, ask for the intended input and consequential failure, and record one primary endpoint and the minimum correction worth pursuing. Then show the aggregate results. A blinded discussion with the supervisor is sufficient to set the direction. For claims about clinical usefulness, identify an appropriately qualified reader and a reproducible evaluation design. Once the question is fixed, the next investment is a matched published comparator and a prespecified replication on fresh patient-linked cases or another seed, followed by one locked external comparison. Another seed tests optimization variability; a fresh patient cohort tests generalization. The pair serve different purposes.

**Further work is technically possible today.** Named-model replication, more seeds, alternate architectures and external inference are all feasible subject to access and compute. The strongest reason to pause those now is the unresolved scientific endpoint. The [blinded review packet](runs/ablation-fold0/contrast-case-review/blinded-cases.pdf) and [response form](runs/ablation-fold0/contrast-case-review/response-sheet.csv) are ready for that decision.
