# Can the reference change which contour scores better?

Development experiment, 23 November 2026. Use all 76 distinct decoded training scans with two different lossless binary masks in the verified TN-SCUI2020 mirror. The previous audit established membership and measured mask overlap. The following choices precede scoring the model comparison. One scan contributes one pair, irrespective of its two release IDs. Grader identity, acquisition history and annotation version are unknown.

## Target correspondence before model inference

Order scans by decoded image digest and order the two IDs numerically within each pair. Verify identical native grayscale arrays and distinct native 0/255 masks. Compute foreground Dice and inspect an image with both reference contours for every pair, without scores or predictions. Flag native Dice below .75, either centroid outside the other mask, or a substantial component (at least 5% of its mask area, eight-connected) with under 10% of its own area overlapping the other mask. A visual review can also flag a suspected different target, uncertain correspondence or conspicuously different target extent. Keep all flagged pairs in a separate stratum even if superficially plausible. Describe the remaining pairs as apparent same-target cases. This screen does not establish reader identity or clinical correctness. Record class-label conflicts separately.

## Paired comparison

Run the frozen TN3K fold-0 grayscale U-Net with flip averaging once per scan. Use the whole image with a 224-pixel long side, remove padding, threshold at .5, and erode that binary prediction once with the same four-connected SciPy operation as in the earlier TN3K diagnostic. Resize the two masks separately with nearest-neighbor interpolation. The primary metric is surface Dice within two evaluation-grid pixels. Region Dice is secondary. Include every scored scan, including missed targets; also describe the apparent same-target subset localized at baseline under both references (region Dice at least .5). Record an empty resized reference as unscorable.

For each reference compute 100 times (eroded score minus original score). Classify each pair as both positive, both negative, opposite signs, or involving a tie (absolute change below 1e-9 points). For opposite signs, the smaller absolute change is the strength of the reversal. Call a reversal material if both changes have magnitude at least two points; tabulate one- and five-point cutoffs as sensitivity checks. Two points is a study convention. Prioritize independent tracing if at least five apparent same-target scans show a material reversal; this is a working decision for this project. Report every case's two changes, distribution and largest illustrative example. Describe these 76 selected scans as a finite set; population prevalence and average performance on the full release require different sampling.

Reusable examples for the meeting: strongest material reversal on apparent same-target scans if present, most concordant benefit or loss (by minimum absolute change), and lowest reference overlap in the flagged stratum.
