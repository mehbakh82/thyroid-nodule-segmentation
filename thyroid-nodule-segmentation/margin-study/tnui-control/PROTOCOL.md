# Reference-format control on a second dataset
23 November 2026. Select 48 image/mask pairs solely by SHA-256 ordering of "tnui-codec-v1/" plus filename from the TNUI-2021 author training partition at 5fa417629f581d82017eed535cdd62586717b669. The purpose is a development experiment on reference encoding. Confirm native dimension pairing, a black background and exactly one positive grayscale mask value. Exclude unsupported cases with their reasons. PNG is a lossless container; its presence alone says nothing about upstream contour editing, patient independence or independent readers.

On each eligible image run the frozen TN3K grayscale ResNet34 U-Net with horizontal flip averaging, 0.5 threshold and the original aspect-preserving 224-pixel grid. Use grayscale inputs and zero padding. On CPU only. Keep the prediction and its one-pixel binary erosion fixed. Primary comparison on all eligible images: change in two-pixel surface Dice after erosion when evaluated against (a) the released binary mask and (b) that same mask deliberately JPEG-compressed at native size and decoded keeping only 255. A third arm decodes the same JPEG at >127. Compress the binary 0/255 reference with the most frequent luminance quantization table among TN3K training/validation masks. Make no adjustment after reading TNUI results. Report equal image means, direction changes and conditional paired image-bootstrap intervals; duplicate decoded images count once. A rate of clinical errors or a patient-level generalization estimate is outside this design. DDTI and all official test partitions are reserved from model scoring. Locally compare image identities to TN3K development before treating them as new content.
