# Local correction of thyroid nodule margins — provisional development protocol

**23 November 2026.** Assume the target nodule is already localized, retaining an accurate contour matters, clinical calipers are incidental, and the initial deployment input is an unprompted B-mode field. These are working assumptions for discussion with Dr. Amiri. The study asks whether a correction within four pixels of a predicted boundary improves contour agreement while protecting pixels already correct under two defensible readings of the annotation.

Use all 576 images from the existing TN3K fold-0 development validation set. The source segmentation network was trained on the separate 2,303-image portion. Freeze the grayscale checkpoint and use the average foreground probability of the original and horizontally flipped views. Confirm the baseline against the previously recorded CPU flip results. The low-cost trainable component is a local pixel classifier; the segmentation network stays fixed.

## Methods fixed before examining the results

- Baseline is grayscale flip averaging, threshold 0.5. Only pixels within four Euclidean pixels of its foreground/background interface inside the unpadded image are editable. Empty and full initial masks are returned as received. Process all images, irrespective of the unknown reference or localization quality.
- Classical comparator: four iterations of morphological geodesic active contour, Gaussian edge indicator with sigma 1 and alpha 100 on grayscale scaled to 0–1, one smoothing step and zero balloon force. Clip its changes to the same predicted band.
- Learned inputs: two-view mean probability, absolute flip disagreement, signed distance to the initial boundary, grayscale, Gaussian mean and local standard deviation, gradient magnitude at sigma 1 and 2, and outward/inward grayscale samples two pixels from each site along the baseline normal. No reference contour, reference visibility, file name or patient ID enters the predictors.
- Use two histogram gradient boosting classifiers, one for each reference decoding (>127 and ==255). Use the same sampled training locations for each, with at most 192 band pixels per image sampled uniformly by a fixed seed. Fit 70 iterations, learning rate 0.08, at most 15 leaves, minimum 20 samples per leaf, L2 regularization 1, early stopping off. Each sampled image has equal total fit weight. Compare a single primary-label classifier at 0.5, the mean of the two classifiers at 0.5, and selective correction: change a background pixel only if both foreground probabilities are at least 0.70, and remove a foreground pixel only if both are at most 0.30. All other pixels retain the original label.
- Five image-level folds formed by sorting a salted SHA-256 of filenames and distributing round-robin. Each validation image is evaluated once with local classifiers fitted on the other folds. No search over features, parameters, gating thresholds or contour widths. The 576 images informed previous design decisions and lack verified patient groups; this is exploratory cross-fitted development evidence.
- Primary comparison: paired change in surface Dice at two pixels for selective correction versus unchanged baseline among images with baseline region Dice at least 0.50 on the >127 reference. Fix this evaluation subset for every method and decoder. Also compare with the generic two-head mean. Secondary: all-image region Dice, surface Dice, HD95 and complete misses; at 1/2/3 pixels under both decoders, count corrected and newly incorrect pixels where both reference definitions agree, and count images with improvements or deteriorations of at least five surface-Dice points. Bootstrap paired image-level differences 5,000 times, seed 241123.
- Provisional investment criteria: advance this version only if the primary mean gain is at least two surface-Dice points with an interval above zero, the mean gain is positive under both decodings at two pixels, and it corrects more reference-stable pixels than it damages. Two points is a working research threshold. Clinical significance and expert contour correctness require reader input. Interpret outcomes against the simpler refiners and do not tune a failed gate on this cohort.

The pilot tests sensitivity to local grayscale evidence and annotation encoding. The two encodings are technical variants of one annotation. A second independent reader, verified patient grouping, and a subsequent frozen external test serve different purposes.
