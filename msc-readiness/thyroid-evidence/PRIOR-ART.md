# Prior art and competitive baselines

The useful comparison is between experiments answering the same question. Within-site segmentation, source-only transfer, target adaptation using unlabeled images, box-prompted segmentation and longitudinal cine segmentation each give the algorithm different information. Scores below are the original authors' unless identified as a calculation from a released table.

## Direct competitors to the proposed thesis

| Work | What it establishes | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Gong et al., TRFE-Net/TRFE-Net++ | The TN3K creators already use anatomical gland context and adaptive feature enhancement to address confusion between nodules and other structures. Their experiments include DDTI. | Include a gland-aware reference when asserting that spatial context fixes false positives. Author code includes train/eval and dataset loaders. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2022.106389) · [Code](https://github.com/haifangong/TRFE-Net-for-thyroid-nodule-segmentation) |
| MRDB | A ResNet/Mamba dual-branch model reports 0.9002 Dice on TN3K and 0.7496 on DDTI in its external test. The paper also evaluates TNUI-2021. | Direct prior art for TN3K training followed by DDTI testing. Boundary-aware loss and architecture are also established. [Methods and Table 4](https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering11101047) |
| DSANet | Reports 79.10% TN3K internal and 73.50% DDTI transfer Dice, evaluating TN3K-trained models without DDTI fine-tuning. Shows an example of adjacent nodules being merged. | Use as a transfer and lightweight-model comparator. A qualitative illustration cannot tell us how frequent a failure is. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.imavis.2026.106018) · [Figure 13 and failure analysis](https://cronfa.swan.ac.uk/Record/cronfa72393/Download/72393__37401__572c97f646a04cc681264222705fe396.pdf) |

The paper also contains mixed-dataset experiments and boundary metrics. | Particularly close competitor. Extract the source-only Table 9 row. The reported mixed-benchmark figures answer a different question. Confirm averaging before comparing mIoU and Dice. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2026.1803455) |
| Prochazka, Zeman and Smutek | Public repository for an **under-review** study reports 2,160 runs, six thyroid datasets, three architectures and 30 transfer directions. Includes fixed manifests, per-image results, augmentation-selection rules and aggregate results. | Strong augmentation is a necessary comparator. Verify which DDTI derivative and unit the authors evaluated. [Pinned repository](https://github.com/antproc/thyroid-ultrasound-augmentation-benchmark/tree/eeb752dd67335d45a2f45a94516c59c63989a3d4) |

Synthetic augmentation adds compute and another source of variation. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jbi.2025.104963) |

### What a strong augmentation baseline buys

Below are released mean Dice values for TN3K → DDTI. The authors evaluated **86 panels from 76 DDTI cases** and three seeds. Their broader case map has 380 cases, 70 with multiple panels. We checked the distinct case IDs and case-disjoint partitions in the released manifest. The 86 panels cannot be substituted for all 637 masks in a paper-to-paper comparison.

| Architecture | Baseline | Validation-selected single augmentation | TrivialAugment | RandAugment |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| ResNet34–U-Net | .602 | .627 | .655 | .648 |
| DeepLabV3+ | .592 | .618 | .681 | .658 |
| MiT-B2–U-Net | .648 | .679 | .667 | .699 |

The .0535 absolute difference between ResNet34–U-Net baseline and TrivialAugment gives the scale of one credible competing intervention under this exact protocol. Selecting the best column on DDTI for our model would tune on the test cohort. Use a source-selected policy and preserve a final cohort outside development. ## The four supervisor papers in context

- **HFA-UNet** provides the most informative starting point for the reverse direction and Stanford transfer. Its three-skip variant achieves .8456 internally on TN3K and .7018 when trained on DDTI and applied to TN3K. Public inference applies postprocessing that includes morphological closing and optionally filters connected components. Report pre- and postprocessing for comparisons involving isolated misses and multiple nodules. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knosys.2025.114245) · [Published prediction code](https://github.com/RosieYY628/HFA-UNet/blob/fd013270a439e989c2c6741831368ac8bcd246a5/predict.py)
- **TEM-Net** is the closest control for raw grayscale, local contrast enhancement and gradient magnitude. Its best listed comparator trails by 0.59 Dice points on TN3K and 0.47 on DDTI in the reported internal experiments. Seed variability and clinical-case uncertainty address different questions. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering13070810)
- **RCGA-UNet-DS** supplies useful over- and under-segmentation endpoints. Preserve the discrepancy between its DDTI fixed-split table and five-fold methods/results when reconstructing comparisons. Its reported external experiment used breast ultrasound. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2026.110686)
- **WAU-Net** provides split files and explicit pixel HD95. On its own TN3K split, Dice was .7889 versus .7888 for ERFNet, while ERFNet's HD95 was lower. Its public evaluation assigns the image diagonal for a one-empty-mask case; excluding those failures would yield a different distance average. Its cross-dataset experiment used breast cohorts. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2026.111478) · [Evaluator](https://github.com/apple1986/WAU-Net/blob/096301fdaa1ee5caff08c33a91e8558b6af5cfaa/evaluate_metrics.py)

## Other method families worth understanding

| Family | Most relevant evidence | Thesis implication |
|---|---|---|
| Appearance, shape and boundary supervision | Echogenicity- and texture-guided active contours predate neural segmentation. TNSNet uses shape supervision. BPAT-UNet preserves boundaries. A contrast-enhanced VM-UNet combines wavelet features, edge-mask learning and contrastive loss. | A Sobel channel, boundary loss, multi-scale encoder, or attention block alone has extensive prior art. Compare explicit edges with both a grayscale control and a competent published edge-aware approach. [Classical contours](https://doi.org/10.1109/TITB.2008.2007192) · [TNSNet](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmpb.2021.106600) · [BPAT-UNet](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmpb.2023.107614) · [Contrast-enhanced VM-UNet](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10278-026-01910-6) |
| Calipers and acquisition marks | A 1,278-nodule study reported 90.4% Dice with manual labels and 93.1% with additional caliper guidance. Li et al. built TUD and a marker-inpainting method. | Declare whether calipers are legitimate clinical inputs or unwanted cues. Quantify natural marked/unmarked performance before interpreting artificial erasure. Neutral controls should remove equally sized non-marker areas without using the nodule mask at inference. [Caliper guidance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2019.10.003) · [TUD and inpainting](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2023.12.011) |
| Domain shift and adaptation | Bidirectional image translation has been studied for cross-site thyroid segmentation; federated learning has been studied with images from 17 hospitals. | A target-adaptation experiment that consumes external images needs its own training protocol and separate target test. Federated learning needs real institutional partitions to support a clinical-site claim. [Cross-site adaptation](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2023.1055815) · [Federated learning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neunet.2024.106754) |
| Alternative supervision | Weak supervision using foreground/background signals and bounding-box supervision, and jointly learning segmentation and classification, already exist. DiFF explicitly models inter-reader uncertainty across tasks including thyroid nodules. | A thesis about reader ambiguity needs individual reader masks or fresh readings. An adjudicated reference, a diagnostic label and two encodings of the same JPEG cannot measure inter-reader disagreement. [FBN](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2023.04.009) · [Box supervision](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compbiomed.2025.109669) · [Multitask learning](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.media.2022.102443) · [DiFF](https://doi.org/10.1109/TMI.2024.3394045) |
| Pretraining, prompts and automation | SAMUS/AutoSAMUS use a compiled ultrasound benchmark. Its MICCAI supplement places TN3K in training, validation and testing, and lists DDTI's 637 masks under testing. UltraSam compiles a broader ultrasound corpus that includes thyroid sources. Masked-autoencoder pretraining has also been evaluated on Stanford, TN3K and DDTI. | An oracle point or box from a test mask gives extra information. Compare free-running models together, and human-prompted or oracle-prompted methods in separately labeled experiments. Check the exact checkpoint's training data. Dataset inclusion in an aggregate alone does not prove a particular image was trained on. [US30K split](https://papers.miccai.org/miccai-2024/supp/1336_supp.pdf) · [SAMUS code](https://github.com/xianlin7/SAMUS) · [UltraSam](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16222) · [MAE thyroid study](https://doi.org/10.3389/frai.2025.1618426) |
| Video and multi-instance evaluation | Cine segmentation and multicenter video instance segmentation with explicit detection-rate evaluation have already been studied. | A model swimming between structures across a video is a different failure from a 2-D weak edge. If switching to video, define temporal consistency, missing instances and patient-level sampling explicitly. [Recurrent cine segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2020.3045906) · [Multicenter instance segmentation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2025.09.015) |
| Evaluation and reporting | Metrics Reloaded motivates selecting metrics from the task and failure cost. CLAIM calls for transparent partitioning, reference standards, acquisition and test definitions. | Pair Dice with a spatial boundary measure and a complete-miss count. State how multiple objects and empty predictions are scored and how missing spacing is handled. [Metrics Reloaded](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-02151-z) · [CLAIM 2024](https://doi.org/10.1148/ryai.240300) |
