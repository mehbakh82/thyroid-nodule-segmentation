# Thesis evidence brief — thyroid nodule segmentation on B-mode ultrasound

**Decision, 21 November 2026.** Proceed with TN3K and DDTI as Dr. Amiri proposed. Use the period before the meeting to establish one reproducible baseline and a catalog of its development errors. My leading thesis question is **when explicit edge information improves weak-margin contours across acquisitions, and when suppressing that information helps because speckle or measurement marks supply a stronger edge than the tissue does**. The data support testing that question. A beneficial intervention and a novel contribution remain to be demonstrated.

## Five findings that change the design

**1. The proposed transfer direction already has substantial prior art.** Gong et al., MRDB, DSANet and DPF–EHDNet all evaluate aspects of TN3K/DDTI transfer. The last three explicitly test TN3K-trained models on DDTI. TEM-Net already supplies raw, contrast-enhanced and gradient channels. Cross-dataset evaluation and adding an edge channel are reproducible starting points. The opportunity is to isolate which real errors are corrected, against a strong source-only baseline, on cases grouped and scored consistently. [MRDB](https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering11101047) · [DPF–EHDNet](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2026.1803455) · [TEM-Net](https://doi.org/10.3390/bioengineering13070810)

**2. A label-loading decision changes the reference most for small nodules.** All 3,493 paired TN3K masks in the author archive decoded to a maximum value of 255 and contained intermediate gray levels. The published TRFE loader divides by each mask's maximum then casts to integer, retaining only pixels equal to 255 for this archive. RCGA-UNet-DS describes binarizing at >127. On the 2,879 train/validation masks, median agreement between these rules was **0.9434**. Among the smallest 720 nodules by relative mask area it was **0.8766**, and among the largest 720 it was **0.9727**. Thresholds >63 and >191 each had median agreement of 1.000 with >127. These are native-grid, same-file comparisons. The measured discrepancy cannot be subtracted from a model score or attributed to radiologist disagreement. We have not shown that it reverses model rankings. [Published loader](https://github.com/haifangong/TRFE-Net-for-thyroid-nodule-segmentation/blob/a2f11b4f3e5cb79b85d1472b6ba489499eef47e7/dataloaders/tn3k.py) · [RCGA protocol](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bspc.2026.110686) · [Local sensitivity results](mask-sensitivity.json)

**3. DDTI needs a version and a counting unit.** In a checked third-party 256 × 256 package, 1,274 image filenames yield 637 mask-matched entries. All 637 extra filenames are exact decoded-pixel aliases. The 637 labeled entries themselves contain **630 distinct RGB arrays** and **634 distinct image/binary-mask pairs**. Four same-image groups have differing masks. One exact image identity crosses the mirror's supplied train/test lists. Those findings apply to that mirror. The authors' respective code and lists would be needed to attribute them to a specific paper. In a separately documented XML-based benchmark, 466 panels represent 380 cases. Its 86 external test panels correspond to 76 cases. These releases represent the same dataset family; counts cannot be pooled as independent observations. [Audited mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/us-segmentator/us-segmentation-dataset/tree/6e7dadb8681fa5a137a1a607127510e36235610d/zips/Thyroid) · [File audit](file-audit.json) · [Case manifest and benchmark check](benchmark-check.json)

**4. A low-contrast margin and a weak image gradient are related but distinct.** I tested a simple physical intuition on TN3K train/validation only. I resized each image proportionally to a 256-pixel long side, computed a Gaussian gradient magnitude (sigma 1 pixel), and compared locations on the supplied >127 contour with points 3 pixels inside and outside along its normal. In **1,412/2,879 images (49.0%)**, the median contour response exceeded both control medians. For the median image, **36.6%** of sampled boundary locations had a stronger response than both controls. The comparison used the reference mask to locate the boundary, so it is an offline descriptive measurement. Its dependence on smoothing, resizing and the chosen contour limits interpretation. It measures neither model failure nor clinician-rated margin visibility. A deterministic visual check of 24 development thumbnails also found unambiguous caliper crosses or measurement lines in at least five. That is a spot check, not an estimated prevalence. [Probe code](edge_probe.py) · [Measurements](development-edge-probe.json) · [Earlier evidence on caliper guidance](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ultrasmedbio.2019.10.003)

**5. Compare with augmentation before crediting a new module.** In an under-review six-dataset benchmark, the published ResNet34–U-Net TN3K → DDTI mean Dice rises from 0.6016 to 0.6551 with TrivialAugment. DeepLabV3+ goes from 0.5922 to 0.6806 under that policy. I checked the released aggregate CSV and case manifest. These numbers use that benchmark's preparation and acknowledge its under-review status. A different independent two-institution study reported attention U-Net Dice above 0.91 at both institutions. The defensible premise is that *specific transfer settings and cases* expose correctable errors. [Public benchmark](https://github.com/antproc/thyroid-ultrasound-augmentation-benchmark/tree/eeb752dd67335d45a2f45a94516c59c63989a3d4) · [Institutional study](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11548-024-03294-w)

## Proposed experiment for discussion

| Step | Concrete choice | What would change the research decision |
|---|---|---|
| Establish the intended case | Ask whether the input is a full field with no prompt, an already selected nodule image, or an image with clinical calipers present and permitted. Agree on the consequence of a missed nodule, boundary overshoot and missed second nodule. | These imply different populations, reference masks and endpoints. Neither TN3K nor this DDTI mirror establishes false-alert performance on routine nodule-negative exams. |
| Freeze the reference | Use >127 at the native resolution for the thesis primary binary label and nearest-neighbor mask resampling. Retain a separate reproduction setting for the max-only loader. Review raw/grayscale/decoded contour overlays with Dr. Amiri. Distinguish multiple labeled lesions from repeated views of one lesion. | If apparent gains disappear or change sign under a reasonable label convention, report dependence on that convention. Verified clinician contours would be needed to establish anatomical superiority. |
| Establish a deployment-matched baseline | Train a 2-D ResNet34–U-Net with source-selected ultrasound-appropriate augmentation. Reproduce one stronger reference, such as the challenge nnU-Net or HFA-UNet, on the same source split and scorer. Keep image field of view, resolution, loss, seeds, optimizer, training budget and postprocessing comparable. Split before augmentation. Start at 256 pixels, profile memory and use mixed precision or gradient accumulation within the shared 24 GB allocation. | Use actual per-case failures to decide whether edges, localization or reference ambiguity is the main bottleneck. Small published Dice differences do not establish sufficient headroom. |
| Isolate the mechanism | First compare replicated grayscale, grayscale plus contrast, plus gradient, and the full fixed three-channel input. Vary one factor per comparison. A learned edge-reliability gate is justified only if edge inputs fix some failures but predictably worsen others. Compare it with a capacity-matched generic attention control. Include a perturbation control for marker suppression and its inpainting artifacts. | If the fixed input or augmentation explains the gain, the simpler method is the result. The primary deployment path consumes image information available at inference. Define difficulty from ground truth only for blinded evaluation strata. |
| Evaluate on independent units | On source development, count complete misses and overshoot/undershoot; prespecify relative-size and contrast groups and how the image border/measurement marks are handled. Hold DDTI predictions for a frozen comparison. Use Dice plus a prespecified boundary measure and return a defined penalty for an empty prediction. Preserve aspect ratio, report the grid and units, and require verified spacing before reporting mm. | Patient/case-paired differences and cluster bootstrap intervals; repeated seeds measure optimization variability. If only image IDs are recoverable, state that unit and use a separate patient-linked cohort for the stronger inference. |
| Decide whether to continue | Set a worthwhile improvement with Dr. Amiri using representative masks and, if possible, reader differences or editing burden. Estimate paired variance and the eligible difficult-case count before a major training campaign. Require evidence that the improvement survives the strong baseline and a second acquisition setting. | Illustrative planning only: with paired difference SD 0.15 and 76 independent cases, the usual 95% half-width is about 0.034 Dice. About 217 independent cases would be needed for ±0.02 under those assumptions. Subgroups have fewer units. Use the actual pilot variance and grouping when available. |

A **second reader** on a modest, prespecified set would be particularly valuable for ambiguous boundaries. A clinician could separately label boundary visibility and contour the cases while blinded to model differences. If reader time is unavailable, use a single-reference segmentation study and describe the uncertainty it leaves. A downstream claim about reporting time, diagnostic improvement or reduced repeat imaging requires its own corresponding measurements.

## What to bring to Dr. Amiri

1. Two examples from each of three failure types once baseline predictions exist: missed object, leakage into nearby thyroid tissue, and correct localization with an inaccurate boundary. Show how many independent cases each type represents. Ask which error matters most in her intended application.
2. Examples of JPEG mask decoding, visible calipers and cropped panels. Confirm which data distribution and reference she wants as primary and whether she knows the source of the 637-image DDTI release used by her group.
3. A choice of external evidence: ThyroidXL for still images, TN-SCUI for one-image-per-patient material, TUD for marker mechanisms, or an Iranian collaborator with usable contours. Decide which additional resource answers the failure question before requesting data.

- Scope is human thyroid-nodule delineation on B-mode US. Gland segmentation, detection, diagnostic classification, elastography and video were retained as neighboring tasks only when they change the choice of data, comparator or claim.
