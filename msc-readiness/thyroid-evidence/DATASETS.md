# Thyroid ultrasound datasets for an MSc segmentation thesis

**Recommended portfolio.** Use TN3K for development and a specified DDTI release for transfer. For an additional still-image cohort, prioritize ThyroidXL if its terms and labels suit the study. Use the publicly described TN-SCUI training portion as the alternative with explicit patient accounting. Use Stanford when video consistency becomes part of the research question.

| Collection | Evidence and intended role | Limitation or access decision |
|---|---|---|
| **TN3K** | Author archive examined. 2,879 train/validation and 614 official test images, all paired with masks. Published cohort comprises 2,421 patients. Five supplied train/validation folds cover all development indices once in validation. | Released filenames, index folds and two-column image/class CSVs do not establish patient identity. Original folds, official test, and WAU-Net's 699-image test serve different comparisons. Code MIT license applies to code. Record data terms separately. [Author deposit](https://huggingface.co/datasets/haifan-gong/TN3K) · [Author repository](https://github.com/haifangong/TRFE-Net-for-thyroid-nodule-segmentation) |
| **DDTI / TDID, Colombia** | A family of exports of the Universidad Nacional/IDIME collection. Original annotations include case material and XML. All four supervisor papers describe 637 images. In the audited 256 × 256 mirror, 637 labeled entries correspond to 630 unique decoded images. | For direct paper reproduction, identify that paper's exact files, mask convention and split. For patient/case-grouped analysis, the documented XML panel map is a better starting point. Keep the grouping label as *case* until patient correspondence is demonstrated. The original provider page could not be retrieved during this audit. [Pedraza et al.](https://doi.org/10.1117/12.2073532) · [Audited mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/us-segmentator/us-segmentation-dataset/tree/6e7dadb8681fa5a137a1a607127510e36235610d/zips/Thyroid) · [Case-aware preparation](https://github.com/antproc/thyroid-ultrasound-augmentation-benchmark/blob/eeb752dd67335d45a2f45a94516c59c63989a3d4/configs/datasets/ddti.yaml) |

No local clinical access is assumed. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.3233/SHTI220482) · [Authors' data-access statement](https://pure.amsterdamumc.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/152255586/Thyroid-ultrasound-image-dataset.pdf) |
| **TN5000 / TN5K** | The original 5,000-image resource provides pathology-related classes and bounding boxes and documents crosshair presence. It could support a marker-prevalence or detector study. | Require documented pixel-reference provenance for any segmentation result. One later collection explicitly lists SAM2-generated masks. ## How to choose the third cohort

For automatic segmentation of still images, choose a release with human contours, a defensible grouping unit, and documented acquisition and marker policy. ThyroidXL best fits those criteria among the additions inspected. TN-SCUI is attractive when exposure to a pretrained ultrasound model can be ruled out. TUD addresses a specific artifact question. Stanford addresses a temporal one. A small well-described local sample with two contour readers could answer a question about human agreement that thousands of single-reference images leave unresolved.

Confirm data-use and redistribution terms with each provider when obtaining files. The committed review artifacts contain aggregate measurements, metadata and scripts. Source archives and thumbnails were inspected locally. [Source revisions and archive digests](sources.json)
